## Context

The existing model is generated by `models/classic-f2b/build_classic.py`. The supplied raster plan uses broken views and inconsistent scale. Three local photographs show the same livery, opposite aircraft views, and a detailed cockpit. The user approved this bounded correction and its implementation.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Preserve explicit plan dimensions and improve visible shape agreement. Produce an editable model with packed references and reviewable comparisons. Do not claim exact unseen geometry, original airfoil coordinates or manufacturing accuracy.

## Decisions

- Retain dimensional wing, tail, hinge and engine-yaw constraints. Use shape-preserving interpolation between existing fuselage and wing-tip stations.
- Place the canopy crest approximately 42% aft of its nose and recess its sill, interpreting the dimensioned 65 mm rise relative to the fuselage deck rather than the lowest sill point.
- Fit a perspective camera to each overall photograph, using only wing, tail and spinner landmarks, and triangulate features seen in both photographs. Where both photographs contradict the plan, follow the photographed aircraft, because its livery is the model's texture:
  - main gear: 450 mm track, legs leaving the wing just behind its leading edge, teardrop spats;
  - fin: straight swept leading edge, aft top corner, near-vertical trailing edge.
  The fin's 125 mm height above the aft deck is retained.
- Open a cockpit well so the seat and controls are visible; use the close-up photograph for the reclined perforated seat.
- Run all geometry creation and verification in Blender's standalone background process. No IDE or libGDX thread is involved.
- Validate the saved file, not only generator parameters. Compare independent world-space dimensions and manifold geometry, and inspect fresh renders beside the unmodified reference photographs.

## Risks / Trade-offs

Photo perspectives differ and contain reflections baked into the existing projected livery. Similar-angle renders permit qualitative comparison, not calibrated photogrammetry. The raster's incompatible scales prevent exact agreement with every pixel; explicit dimensions take priority. Cockpit and fin contour details remain estimates. Triangulation uses hand-marked silhouette extremes and cameras with ~20 px landmark residuals. Spinner and canopy-nose checks agree within ~5 mm, but height is uncertain to about ±15 mm. Overriding the plan's 370 mm track trades agreement with the drawing for agreement with the photographed, textured aircraft.
